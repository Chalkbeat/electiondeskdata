## Full text

The Federal Voting Assistance Program (FVAP.gov) is the Department of Defense program that helps uniformed service members, their eligible family members, and overseas citizens vote absentee under UOCAVA (52 U.S.C. §§ 20301 et seq.). The site provides the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA), the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), state-by-state absentee voting guidance, and deadlines. The site is a JavaScript application that cannot be captured as static text — consult https://www.fvap.gov/ directly for current forms, deadlines, and state guidance.
