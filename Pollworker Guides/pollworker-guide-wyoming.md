GENERAL VOTING INFORMATION 
VOTING IN-PERSON – VOTER ID REQUIREMENTS 
Voters are required to show an acceptable form of identification when voting in person. Any of the 
following will be accepted: 
 
• WY Driver's License or ID Card 
• Tribal ID Card 
• Valid U.S. Passport 
• U.S. Military Card 
• Driver’s License or ID Card from Another 
State 
 
• University of Wyoming Student Photo ID 
• Wyoming Community College Student Photo ID 
• Wyoming Public School Student Photo ID 
• Valid Medicare or Medicaid Insurance Card 
• Valid Wyoming Concealed Firearm Permit 
VOTING ABSENTEE 
If you are a registered voter, you may request an absentee ballot at any time during the calendar year in 
which the election is held but not on the day of the election. Absentee voting begins, and ballots are 
distributed, 28 days before the election.   
To request an absentee ballot, contact your County Clerk. You will need to provide your full name, 
residence address, mailing address, and date of birth. Remember to ensure that your absentee ballot is 
returned to your County Clerk’s office by 7 p.m. on Election Day. 
 
Military & Overseas Citizen Voting (UOCAVA)  
If you are an active duty member of the Armed Forces, Merchant Marine, Public Health Service, or NOAA 
Commissioned Corps, an eligible family member of covered military personnel, or a U.S. citizen residing 
outside the United States, you may be eligible to vote under the Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee 
Voting Act (UOCAVA). UOCAVA voting begins, and ballots are distributed, 45 days before the election. To 
request a UOCAVA ballot, contact your County Clerk by phone, mail, email, or in person, or download a 
request form at https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Elections/General/AbsenteeRequestForm.pdf. Remember to 
ensure that your completed ballot is returned to your County Clerk's office by 7 p.m. on Election Day.  
 
VOTING ASSISTANCE 
Wyoming State law allows ONE person to assist a voter in the following situations: blindness, disability, or 
inability to read or write. This person cannot be the voter’s employer, an agent of the voter’s employer, or 
an agent or officer of the voter’s union. Please contact your County Clerk with any questions about polling 
place accessibility. 
 
BALLOT PROPOSITIONS (CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS, INITIATIVES, AND 
REFERENDA)  
Statewide ballot propositions appear on the General Election Ballot. In order to pass, they must be 
approved by a majority of those voting in the General Election.  
 
SAMPLE BALLOTS 
Sample ballots are published in local newspapers and are available at your County Clerk’s office.

VOTER REGISTRATION INFORMATION 
IT’S EASY TO REGISTER TO VOTE 
You may register and vote at your polling place on Election Day.  
You may also register to vote in person at the County Clerk’s office or by mail not less 
than (14) days before an election. The voter registration application may be found on 
the Secretary of State’s website at: https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Elections/General/
VoterRegistrationForm.pdf   
 
VOTER REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS 
 Be a U.S. Citizen, as evidenced by providing proof of U.S. citizenship*  
 Be 18 years of age on or before the day of the next election 
 Be a resident of Wyoming, as evidenced by providing proof of Wyoming residency*  
 Be a bona fide resident of Wyoming for not less than thirty (30) days before the next 
election 
 Not currently adjudicated mentally incompetent 
 Not been convicted of a felony, or have had voting rights restored 
 Provide your Wyoming Driver’s License or Driver’s License number and acceptable 
identification. If you do not have a Wyoming Driver’s License number, provide the 
last four digits of your Social Security Number and another form of acceptable identi-
fication*  
 
*For all forms of I.D. for Registration, proof of Citizenship and proof of Residency see page 5.  
 
KEEP YOUR REGISTRATION CURRENT 
• If you did not vote in the 2024 General Election you should verify your registration with 
your County Clerk. 
• If you moved to a different Wyoming county, you must now register in that county. 
• If you have moved within your county or changed your name since the 2024 Election 
you must notify your County Clerk.  
• If you have questions about the status of your registration, contact your County Clerk. 
 
QUESTIONS ON REGISTERING TO VOTE 
Contact your County Clerk’s office. (Contact information for all 23 County Clerks is    
located on the final two pages of this guide.) 
 
RESTORATION OF VOTING RIGHTS 
If you have been convicted of a felony, you may request to have your voting rights re-
stored.  Contact the Wyoming Department of Corrections at (307) 777-2480, or visit 
their website at: https://corrections.wyo.gov/about-us/restoration-of-rights
