VOTER INSTRUCTIONS 
1. Do not bend or fold your ballot. 
2. V ote for candidate(s) of One (1) party only. If you vote 
on both sides of the ballot, the entire ballot will not be 
counted. 
3. Fill in the oval next to the candidate or candidates of 
your choice. If you choose to write in a candidate, fill 
in the oval and print the candidate’s full name within 
the space provided. 
4. V ote for no more than: 
• One (1) Gubernatorial Team; 
• One (1) Nonvoting Delegate to the U.S. House of 
Representatives; and 
• Up to Fifteen (15) Senators; 
5. If you make a mistake, ask for a new ballot. 
6. When you exit the voting booth, insert your ballot into 
the ballot box.

Who is a Provisional Voter?
1. A person whose name does not appear in the signature roster.
2. A person without an ID. 
3. A person who voted an absentee ballot, but then wishes to vote 
at the polling place on election day.

Processing a Provisional Voter

Reasons a voter might be 
challenged…
• They are not the person whose name appears in 
the Official Voter Signature Roster;
• They are not a resident of Guam;
• They are not a US Citizen;
• They are not a resident of the precinct at which 
they are voting;
• They have already voted absentee/that day;
• They had already voted at another precinct;
• They are registered in another US jurisdiction.
➔ First & foremost call the GEC
➔ The Precinct leader will swear in both the challenger and the voter being challenged.
➔ Officials will have the challenger complete the Entry of Challenge form (EC-14 p29) 
and the voter being challenged the Challenge Rebuttal form (EC-14A p30).
➔ The Challenged voter is given the chance to present witnesses or evidence in support 
of their eligibility to vote.
➔ 3 Precinct officials will address the challenge and resolve the matter on site.
➔ The GEC will convene at 3 p.m. to address any issues that require clarification. 
➔ Absentee voters can be challenged.

Quick Study Guide
1. Poll watchers are not permitted to ask to see the identification of voters.
2. Precinct Officials are only permitted to give ballot instructions in Chamorro or English.
3. If a voter wishes to challenge another voter within their district, the challenge must be 
executed before the voter signs the signature roster. 
4. Voters are not allowed to wear political paraphernalia (i.e. campaign shirts, hats, stickers, 
etc.) inside the polling place.
5. An absentee voter can be challenged at the polls.
6. Voters are only allowed to use blue or black ink pens.
7. Officials may begin defacing ballots after the last voter has been serviced. 
8. EC-12 is the supply checklist, EC-16 is the transfer sheet for ballots/ballot box, and the 
EC-15 is the list of Absentee affidavit envelopes you get in the morning.
