Search Results
The system will display up to 10 potential matches at a time.
If there are more than ten potential matches, you will receive a message to narrow your search criteria.
Results will include both Active and Inactive voters.
Results will include WITHIN County and OUTSIDE of County (if criteria entered match voters outside of your county).
Click anywhere on the voter line to open Voter Details and issue a ballot.


Voter Details
Voter Details will open when the user clicks on the identified voter line in Voter Search.
Voter Details has four sections:
	•	Navigation:
In the upper-right section of the screen there are three navigation buttons.
	•	Back to Results: Takes you back to the current search results.
	•	Previous: Displays the voter record in the Possible Match results that is before the current selected voter record.
	•	Next: Displays the voter record in the Possible Match results that is after the current selected voter record.

	•	Voter Information:
Displays the voter information that was provided on the registration transaction as entered in SCORE, and includes:
	•	Keyline information.
	•	Addresses (residence, mailing, ballot) and Affiliation.
	•	Update Registration Button – Immediately follows voter information section for those users with the VSPC Update Voter role.

	•	Ballot Information:
Displays the history of ballots issued to the voter when there is an active election, and the voter has been issued or voted a ballot.
	•	This section contains the details of all the ballots issued to the voter.
	•	This section will NOT display if the voter was never issued an original mail ballot from your county (i.e., out-of-county voters, inactive voters, new voters, and ineligible voters will not have ballot information displayed).

	•	Issue a Ballot:
Displays buttons for three different ballot types.
	•	When the voter being displayed is eligible for the selected election and they have not already voted the Issue a Ballot section is displayed.
	•	These buttons will be disabled for ineligible voters (e.g., already voted or not in district).

Voter Registration Alerts/Label Indicators
Voter Details may display one or more alerts above the keyline information to describe a voter’s status during the election cycle.

Already Voted:
	•	Indicates the voter already cast their mail ballot or voted in person and is not eligible to receive a second ballot!
	•	When a voter has already voted ePollbook will not allow you to change the voter’s address or issue a ballot.
Confidential – Voter and Confidential – Profession:
	•	Indicates that the voter’s information is confidential.
Inactive Voter:
	•	Indicates the voter’s record is not active. The voter’s record will be made Active when a ballot is issued.
Out of County:
	•	Indicates the voter’s residence is in a different county.
	•	If the voter has moved to your county their registration must be updated to be able to issue a ballot from your county. However, if the voter is not updating their address to your county but wants to vote at the VSPC the voter may be offered a Statewide Ballot.
ID Required:
	•	Indicates the voter must provide acceptable identification before an in-person ballot may be issued.
	•	If the voter wants to receive a mail ballot they will be required to include a copy of their photo ID with their returned mail ballot.

Ballot Information Section
The Ballot Information section provides detailed history of ballots.
As mentioned previously, the “Already Voted” label indicator is a quick, visual way to know that the voter is ineligible to vote another ballot. However, reviewing the voter’s Ballot Information section is equally important.

The Ballot Information section displays the history of a voter's ballot(s). The order of the ballots is from most recent to oldest.
First Column:
	•	Visual indication of the Stage column value.
Method:
	•	The type of ballot the voter was issued.
	•	In-Person ballots are limited to three.
	•	Mail ballots are not limited.
Stage:
	•	Displays the current stage of the ballot. (i.e., sent, voted, undeliverable, spoiled, voided, or undone). 
	•	A voter cannot be issued another ballot if a ballot is in the "voted" stage!
Sent:
	•	The date the mail ballot was sent. In person voters will not have a sent date.
Voted/Received:
	•	The date the voter cast their In-Person ballot or the date the Mail ballot was received.
Location Issued:
	•	Location where the ballot was issued.
Last Column:
	•	Contains a view hyperlink to access the ballot detail pane.

Update a Voter Record Before Issuing a Ballot
The user must have the VSPC Update Voter role to have access to the Update Registration button.
Capturing changes to voter information before issuing the ballot is critical. Updating the registration ensures the voter receives the correct ballot for which they are eligible to vote!
	•	Before issuing the ballot, determine whether the voter’s registration needs to be updated. Compare the registration form or signature card to the information in the voter’s record.
	•	Is there an Affiliation change?
	•	Has the Residential Address changed?
	•	Is there a Name change?
	•	Has the voter added a Driver’s License# or SSN?
	•	Did the voter add, update, or remove a Mailing Address or Ballot Address?

What is the HAVA Check?
HAVA is the acronym for the Help America Vote Act. The HAVA Check’s primary purpose is to confirm the accuracy of the voter registration information and an individual’s eligibility to vote.
The HAVA Check is initiated by the system when information on the keyline is added for a first-time registration or when information on the keyline is being changed/updated for an existing voter record.
Keyline information includes - Name / Date of Birth / Colorado Driver’s License or ID number / Last four of the SSN.
The HAVA Check is activated after entering or updating the keyline information and selecting the Next button.

	•	Identity Verification: If the applicant provides a Colorado Driver’s License or Colorado ID, the voter registration system verifies by matching Name, DOB, CO DL or CO ID against Colorado Department of Revenue (CDOR) records.
	•	Potential Duplicate Voter Records: each voter can only have one active registration. The system searches the voter registration database using Name, DOB, CO DL/CO ID, and Last four SSN to identify additional voter records which may be a duplicate. A duplicate record can be in any status – Active, Inactive, Cancelled, Incomplete, or Pending. If found the existing record will be updated and a second record will not be created.
	•	Deceased Status Indicator: The voter registration system verifies by matching Name, DOB, CO DL/CO ID, and/or Last four SSN against Colorado Department of Health and Environment (CDPHE) records.
	•	Felon Status Indicator: The voter registration system verifies by matching Name, DOB, CO DL/CO ID, and/or Last four SSN against Colorado Department of Corrections (CDOC) records.

The Next button triggers the system to initiate the check. A message will display while the system checks.
