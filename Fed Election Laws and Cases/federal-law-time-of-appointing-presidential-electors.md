# Time of appointing presidential electors — 3 U.S.C. § 1

- **Category:** Presidential elections
- **Authority type:** Statute
- **Citation:** 3 U.S.C. § 1
- **What it is:** Requires electors for President and Vice President to be appointed in each state on Election Day under state law enacted before Election Day.
- **Why useful for reporters / RAG:** Whether a president or state can simply change presidential Election Day; post-election changes to elector-selection rules.

## Full text

§1. Time of appointing electors

The electors of President and Vice President shall be appointed, in each State, on election day, in accordance with the laws of the State enacted prior to election day.
(Added Pub. L. 117–328, div. P, title I, §102(a), Dec. 29, 2022, 136 Stat. 5233 .)
